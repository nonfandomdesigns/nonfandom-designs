ORDER DETAILS

The important stuff before your rug enters production.

Pricing varies based primarily on the complexity of the design.
Turnaround time varies based on the current order backlog and cannot be guaranteed.
A 50% nonrefundable deposit is required to begin production and secure your place in the order queue.
Receive a 3% discount when your order is paid in full upfront.
A $100 expedite fee is available for rush orders and moves your rug to the front of the current order queue.
Discounts are available for returning customers and multiple-rug purchases.
Maximum rug size is 5' × 7'.
Domestic and international shipping is available. Shipping costs are calculated into the final amount.
Final payment is required within 5 days of completion. If payment is not received within that period, the rug will be forfeited and made available for sale.
Rugs are nonrefundable once picked up.
