YOMIZAKURA
CHARACTERS & WORLD LORE
MAIN CHARACTERS
YOMIZAKURA TREE
For thousands of years, hidden in Mount Yoshino in Nara Prefecture, was a dark cherry blossom tree infested by dark spirits.
SAKURAMA
Emerging from the heart of the tree, she's also the spirit of the tree and the manifestation of the tree in devil form.

Yomiten Form

Sakurama’s Yomiten Form is the most powerful form of any character in the universe. She cannot be defeated in this state, but it drains the power of the Yomizakura tree.

KUROGI
The first creation and first seed of Sakurama, her main vessel used.

Cyber Form

Kurogi takes this form when he traverses the cyber future, transforming his tentacles into robotic appendages.

IKO & IKA
The devil twins that were brought to life by Kurogi. They are his children.

1,000-Year Ghost Form

Iko and Ika take this ghost form after Earth has survived an apocalypse.

AOMA
The secret blue devil who is a twin of Kurogi, created by Sakurama in secret from Kurogi. He is represented in the Yomizakura tree by one blue cherry blossom flower.

Wandering Mage Form

After mankind has fallen and little remains of Earth beyond a post-apocalyptic landscape, Aoma takes his Wandering Mage Form. His magic is more powerful in this state.

SAKUYO
The Night Blossom and mask that was created by Kurogi. When I wear it, I'm under his control and do his bidding. When I wear this mask, I make my custom rugs under Kurogi's bidding.
SAKU
A small little demon cherry blossom flower that Sakurama found during her trip to the underworld. Saku serves as a little guide and helper to Sakurama, almost like a shoulder pal.

Diamond Form

Saku hardens her body into diamond, allowing her to travel without worrying about injury or attack.

KUROGUMO-RYUU
The Black Cloud Dragon, discovered during a trip to the underworld by Iko and Ika. They bound it and took control of it, making it their giant dragon pet.

Yomizakura Fusion Form

The devil tree grants Kurogumo Ryuu its power by temporarily fusing with him.

SUPPORTING CHARACTERS
TENZARU
The golden hand and feet macaque monkey who is a semi-omnipotent character whose main focus is to maintain balance.

Omnipotent Gold Form

Tenzaru transforms his body using a rare gold, entering an all-seeing, all-knowing state.

SHIKAYOMI
A yokai who has antlers and a black fox tail. She serves as the main love interest for Kurogi, who is obsessed with her beauty.

Ruin Witch Form

Shikayomi becomes a yokai of complete destruction with four fox tails. Her face turns pure white, and everything she touches turns to black and white flames.

TORAI
Based off a Hyottoko mask, Torai is kind of a fourth wall character who is mostly for comedy purposes. He appears out of nowhere, but doesn't necessarily interact with characters directly. He's simply a fourth wall character.
ANKYU
The guardian of the cherry blossom underworld, with a similar look to Anubis from Egypt. He contains a blue lotus arm and also carries a khopesh sword.
VILLAINS
KOJI & KIRI
Conjoined-head mushroom spore twins who are the main enemies of Iko and Ika.

Full Spore Form

Koji and Kiri fuse their heads, combining their power.

KINHOSHI
Another mushroom spore character whose body also has wood for arms and legs. He carries a tesser with sharp spikes on the ends and is a main enemy to Kurogi.

Full Spore Form

Kinhoshi draws on the leftover residue of cherry blossoms he has destroyed, using the spores to create a shield and strengthen his weapon.

JOROBANA
A giant spider with a mushroom spore abdomen and a Kanzashi hair pin on the abdomen. She serves as the most powerful of the villains and is a main enemy to Kurogumo-Ryuu.
