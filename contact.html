ABOUT THE ARTIST

こんにちは — Hello!

My name is Leo, and I'm the rug maker behind NonFandomDesigns.

I create custom handmade rugs through the bidding of the Yomizakura Tree, wearing the Sakuyo mask as I bring each piece to life. I've been rug making for close to six years, and for me, it has become much more than a craft — it's my outlet for expression and creativity.

Every rug is made by hand with care, personality, and its own story.

Let's create a rug straight from the world of Yomizakura and bring it into your home — something you or someone you love can cherish for years to come.
